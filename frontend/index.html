      How much to trust a reading

      Every score carries a confidence number. It is based on how much of the lake was visible through cloud, and how recently a radar pass saw it. Radar works through cloud, so a cloudy day does not automatically mean low confidence.

      High confidence
      88%

      Fresh radar pass, few clouds. Treat the score as a solid reading.

      Low confidence
      42%

      Old pass, heavy cloud. Treat the score as a prompt to look again — not a verdict.

      What GlacierWatch is not

      • Not a flood clock.It is a decision-support risk indicator, not a prediction of when a lake will burst.
      • Not calibrated for every lake.The physical rules are tuned for ice-dammed surge lakes such as Shisper. Moraine-dammed lakes need separate calibration.
      • Never above local authority.Communities should always follow NDMA and DDMA evacuation orders.
